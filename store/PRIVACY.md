# Privacy Policy for Driving Bible

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Driving Bible is a Singapore driving theory practice app for BTT, FTT, and RTT.

## Data we collect

The app does not create accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Apple privacy nutrition labels for this app report no collected data types and no tracking.

## Network requests

The app loads practice questions over the internet. Opening a handbook loads a PDF in an in-app browser.

Servers involved in those requests may receive standard request metadata such as IP address, as they would for any website visit. Driving Bible does not attach extra personal information to these requests.

Quiz answers stay on your device for the current session. They are not uploaded.
